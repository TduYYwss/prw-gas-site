Put your website on prwgas.com

About 15 minutes, on your laptop. Everything happens in the Cloudflare account where you bought prwgas.com, and it's free.

Before you start: if you added any records earlier that point prwgas.com at GitHub (four A records starting 185.199., or a www record pointing to tduyywss.github.io), delete them first. In Cloudflare go to Domains → prwgas.com → DNS → Records. If you never added them, skip this.

1

Download the website

One file has the whole site in it. Save it to your laptop and leave it zipped.

Download prwgas-website.zip

It's about 1 MB. It ends up in your Downloads folder.

2

Upload it to Cloudflare

  1. Log in at dash.cloudflare.com.
  2. In the left menu, open Workers & Pages. In the newer menu it sits under Compute.
  3. Click Create application, then pick the Pages option.
  4. Choose Drag and drop your files (it may say Upload assets). Don't pick the Git option.
  5. Project name: type prwgas and click Create project.
  6. Drag prwgas-website.zip from your Downloads folder into the box, or click to choose it.
  7. Click Deploy site.

After a few seconds Cloudflare gives you a test address like prwgas.pages.dev. Open it and check the site appears.

If Cloudflare says the name is taken, use something like prwgas-site. It's only the test address; customers will use prwgas.com.

3

Connect prwgas.com

  1. Still in your new prwgas project, open the Custom domains tab.
  2. Click Set up a custom domain, type prwgas.com, then Continue and Activate domain.
  3. Do the same again for www.prwgas.com, so both work.

Because prwgas.com is in the same Cloudflare account, Cloudflare adds the settings for you. It usually takes a few minutes; the screen shows Active when it's done.

4

Check it on your phone

  • Open https://prwgas.com. You should see the padlock in the address bar.
  • Tap Call Paul and WhatsApp to check they open your number.
  • Try the quote form, but don't press send in WhatsApp, or you'll message yourself.

Then send the link to whoever made the site, so they can check it too.

When the website changes

You'll be sent a new prwgas-website.zip. To put it live:

  1. Workers & Pages → your prwgas project.
  2. Click Create deployment (it may say Upload new version).
  3. Drag in the new zip and click Save and deploy.

prwgas.com updates within a minute. Nothing else needs changing.

Once it's live

  • Google profile: use https://prwgas.com as your website.
  • Facebook: add https://prwgas.com to your page's details.
  • Optional email address: in Cloudflare, Email → Email Routing can forward something like info@prwgas.com to your Gmail for free.
Cloudflare sometimes renames its buttons. If something looks different, pick the closest match, or send a screenshot.
