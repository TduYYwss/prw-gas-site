Privacy notice

Last updated 16 November 2026

This explains what personal information PRW Gas Services Ltd collects when you contact us or we work for you, why, and what your rights are. We keep it to what we need to do the job.

Who we are

PRW Gas Services Ltd, registered in Scotland, company number SC833250. We are responsible for your information. Contact Paul Wilkin on 07947 890992 or wilkin434@gmail.com with any questions.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number, email address and postcode or address.
  • Details of the job: your boiler or appliances, faults, and any photos you send us.
  • Records of the work we do, including gas safety records and service reports.
  • For landlords and letting agents: property addresses and tenant contact details where you give them to us to arrange access.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you, quote and carry out the work, because you have asked us to (taking steps towards, or carrying out, a contract with you).
  • To keep records the law requires, such as gas safety records, and records for tax.
  • To remind you when your next service or gas safety check is due, because that is in our and your legitimate interests. You can tell us to stop at any time.
  • To send you occasional offers only if you are a customer who has not opted out, or you have agreed to them. Every message tells you how to stop them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. It is only shared with:

  • the services we use to run the business, such as WhatsApp (Meta), email (Google) and our accountant;
  • boiler manufacturers, if you ask us to register a warranty;
  • anyone we are required to share it with by law, such as the Gas Safe Register or HMRC.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go ahead: deleted within 12 months.
  • Job records, invoices and gas safety records: kept for 6 years, so we have them for tax purposes and in case you need a copy.

This website

The quote and booking forms on this site do not store anything. They open WhatsApp or your email with your message already written, and nothing is sent until you press send. The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we turn on visitor statistics, we use Cloudflare's privacy-friendly version, which does not use cookies or identify you.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how we use it, or stop reminders and offers. Contact us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
