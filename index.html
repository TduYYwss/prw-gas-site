Questions

FAQ

How do I check you're Gas Safe registered?

Search registration number on the Gas Safe Register website, or ask to see my ID card when I arrive. Every legitimate gas engineer carries one.

Do you charge for quotes?

Quotes are free. For repairs I'll tell you any call-out charge before I come out, so there are no surprises.

How often should my boiler be serviced?

Once a year. Most manufacturer warranties require it, and it picks up small faults before they become unsafe or expensive.

I'm a landlord. How often do I need a gas safety check?

Every 12 months by law, and your tenants need a copy of the record. I can remind you when the next one is due.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Don't use switches or naked flames. Open windows, turn the gas off at the meter if it's safe to, get out, and call the National Gas Emergency line on 0800 111 999. Once they've made it safe, get in touch and I'll repair the fault.
